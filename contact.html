Contact the Board

Villas of Worthington is self-managed by its Board of Directors. Reach us by email below.

Email

board@villasofworthingtonhoa.com

This is the address for all Board correspondence, including architectural review (ARC) requests. For ARC requests, use the ARC Request form — it fills in this email for you. You're welcome to email this address directly for anything else, or to send supporting materials (plans, photos, product specs) separately.

What to contact the Board about

Email the Board for things like:

  • Architectural review (ARC) requests
  • Questions about community rules or the governing documents
  • Common-area concerns (landscaping, ponds, the trail, lighting)
  • Maintenance issues on Association-owned property
  • General Association business or questions for the Board

For account balances or dues payments: use the Resident Portal (PayHOA) for the fastest, self-service way to check your account or pay.

For an emergency: call 911 or the appropriate emergency service directly — don't wait on an email reply.
