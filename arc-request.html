Architectural Change Request

Submit this form to the Board before beginning any exterior construction, alteration, or modification.

Under Declaration §9.2.1, the Board — which now holds the original plan-approval authority described there, per §9.2.1.3 — will approve, reject, or modify your request in writing within 30 days after acknowledging receipt of a complete submission. If you don't receive a written response within that time, the request is deemed approved. That doesn't waive compliance, though: any work must still conform to the Declaration, the Design Guidelines, and all applicable City of North Royalton codes and permits.
