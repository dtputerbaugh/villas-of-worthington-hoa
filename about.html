About the Association

Villas at Worthington Homeowners Association, Inc. is an Ohio nonprofit corporation that governs the Villas of Worthington community, a 114-unit planned community (PUD) in North Royalton, Cuyahoga County, Ohio.

Association structure

The Association is governed by an elected Board of Directors under the Association's recorded Declaration of Covenants, Conditions, Restrictions and Reservation of Easements, its Bylaws (Code of Regulations), and the Ohio Planned Community Act (Ohio Revised Code Chapter 5312). The Board adopts and enforces the community's Rules & Regulations and Design Guidelines, and oversees the Association's finances, common elements, and architectural review process.

See the Documents page for the full text of the Declaration, Bylaws, Design Guidelines, and Rules & Regulations.

What the Board does

The Board administers the Association on behalf of all homeowners: overseeing common elements (ponds, the paved trail, entry features, mailboxes, fencing), managing Association finances and the reserve fund, enforcing the governing documents, reviewing architectural change requests, and coordinating maintenance and vendor contracts. Ohio's Planned Community Act (Chapter 5312) sets out this authority and the Board's responsibilities in more detail.

Per the Bylaws (Section 14), Board meetings are open to all Members, although Members other than the Directors may not participate in Board discussion or deliberation unless the Board expressly authorizes it. The Board may adjourn into executive session (Section 15) to discuss matters like personnel or pending litigation, but must first announce the nature of that business in open session.

Self-management

The Association is self-managed by its Board of Directors rather than by an outside management company. Day-to-day communication, assessments, and architectural review requests are handled directly by the Board; see the Contact page for how to reach us.
